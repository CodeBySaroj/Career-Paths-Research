# Technology Career Market Study 2025–2026
## High-value career paths for a computer engineering / IT graduate starting in Nepal

**Prepared:** 9 October 2026  
**Scope:** United States, India and Nepal  
**Decision question:** Which career path gives a new graduate in Nepal the strongest combined prospects for income, international mobility, resilience and growth?

> **Executive answer:** I would choose **backend/distributed-systems engineering with cloud, security and applied AI/MLOps**. It is a better first bet than “prompt engineer” or pure data science because it has the widest employer base, a credible entry route in Nepal and India, strong remote/export potential, and a path into AI infrastructure, platform engineering and security. A strong second choice is cloud/platform engineering with security.

## Executive summary

The market is not one market. The United States offers the highest compensation and the deepest frontier-AI, cloud, semiconductor and security work, but visa and experience barriers are substantial. India offers the best scale of accessible engineering employment and a practical bridge from Nepal to global delivery centres and product companies. Nepal has a smaller market, lower local pay and fewer frontier-research roles, but it is viable for software export, fintech, telecom, QA automation, cloud operations, cybersecurity services and startup work.

Across the three markets, the most portable hiring signal is not a single framework. It is the ability to **build, operate and secure production systems**: programming, SQL, Linux, networking, cloud, containers, observability, testing, system design and clear written English. AI raises the value of this foundation because models still need data pipelines, inference infrastructure, evaluation, security, reliability and product integration.

### The five strongest paths for a Nepal-based graduate

1. **Backend / distributed-systems software engineer + cloud + applied AI** — best overall balance.
2. **Cloud/platform/SRE engineer + DevSecOps** — excellent demand and international mobility; harder first job.
3. **Data engineer + ML platform/MLOps** — strong AI-adjacent route; requires more data and infrastructure depth.
4. **Cybersecurity engineer + cloud security** — durable, regulation-supported and increasingly AI-relevant; entry roles often require evidence of hands-on practice.
5. **Applied ML / generative-AI engineer** — high upside, but fewer true entry-level roles and stronger mathematics/portfolio competition.

### Important caveat on evidence

Official sources publish occupational and company-level information, not a complete comparable vacancy census for 30 employers. Salary data is especially uneven: US public wage data is comparatively strong; India and Nepal figures are mostly reported market ranges and employer-specific disclosures. Where comparable public data is unavailable, this report says **“Reliable public data unavailable”** rather than inventing a number. Company job counts are not used because a careers-page count is volatile, duplicated across locations, and not a measure of hiring demand.

## 1. Methodology and evidence standard

### Research design

* **Company selection:** technology relevance, scale of technical employment, employer stability, product or export exposure, compensation upside, graduate accessibility, international mobility and future relevance. Market capitalisation alone was not used.
* **Role demand:** triangulated from official career portals and role families, O*NET occupation profiles and hot-technology data, public annual reports where relevant, and recurring role patterns in employer postings. Counts below are **company presence counts**, not vacancy counts: a company counts once if the role family is a recurring part of its technical hiring ecosystem.
* **Salary:** US benchmark anchored to O*NET 2025 median wages and reputable market compensation sources; India and Nepal shown as practical market bands assembled from reported compensation data and local hiring observations. Total compensation, base salary and contractor pay are not interchangeable.
* **Currency:** illustrative comparison uses approximately **USD 1 ≈ INR 84 ≈ NPR 135**. Exchange rates move; local offers should be evaluated in local currency and purchasing power.
* **AI assessment:** analytical forecast for 2026–2035, not a prediction of exact headcount.

### Evidence labels

* **Verified:** directly supported by an official or government source linked in Sources.
* **Reported:** compensation or hiring pattern reported by compensation databases, job postings or industry surveys; subject to sampling bias.
* **Estimate / analysis:** reasoned range or score derived from the evidence; not a promise.

## 5. Skills employers repeatedly ask for

### Common core — the highest-return base

* One primary language deeply: **Python** for data/AI/automation; **Java/Go/TypeScript** for service engineering; **C++** for performance, embedded and systems.
* Data structures and algorithms; testing; debugging; Git; code review; API design.
* SQL, relational modelling, transactions and one NoSQL model.
* Linux, shell scripting, processes, memory, networking, HTTP/TLS and DNS.
* Docker, CI/CD, observability, cloud IAM and basic cost awareness.
* Technical writing, incident communication and professional English.

## 6. Education and entry requirements

| Path | Bachelor’s sufficient? | Master’s advantage | Certifications / portfolio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Backend software | Usually yes | Not normally required | Portfolio and internships matter more than certificates |
| Cloud/platform | Yes for many junior roles | No, experience dominates | AWS/Azure/GCP associate can open interviews; lab portfolio essential |
| Data engineering | Yes | Helpful for advanced analytics, not required | SQL/data projects and production pipelines matter |
| Cybersecurity | Yes | Helpful for research/advanced security | Security+, CCNA, cloud-security certs can help; hands-on lab is decisive |
| Applied ML | Yes for applied roles | Significant advantage for research-heavy roles | Reproducible projects, papers/open source, model evaluation |
| AI research | Rarely sufficient | MSc often helpful; PhD common for frontier research | Publications, research code and mathematics matter |
| Embedded/VLSI | Yes | Helpful for advanced semiconductor/R&D | Hardware projects, C/C++, Verilog, verification evidence |
| Product manager | Sometimes | Helpful but not required | Product delivery and domain evidence beat generic certificates |

For a new graduate, **internships, shipped projects and references are the strongest substitutes for missing experience**. A GitHub portfolio cannot waive security clearance, immigration requirements or five years of production ownership, but it can demonstrate the first interview signal.

Competitive programming/DSA is high-value for product-company software interviews in India and the US. It is less important for cloud support, security operations and client-facing roles, where troubleshooting and operational evidence matter more. Publications are valuable for AI research, not necessary for ordinary software, cloud or security roles.

### AI impact, 2026–2035

* **Backend/platform/security:** 🟢 AI-complementary. Boilerplate and routine tickets automate; architecture, reliability, threat models and accountability become more valuable.
* **Data engineering/MLOps:** 🟢 AI-complementary. More AI creates more pipelines, governance, evaluation and serving work.
* **Applied ML/GenAI:** 🟡 AI-transformed. Model access becomes easier; differentiation shifts to evaluation, data, inference cost, safety and domain value.
* **QA automation:** 🟡 AI-transformed. Manual repetitive testing is at risk; test architecture, reliability and production quality remain valuable.
* **Product management:** 🟡 AI-transformed. Discovery and documentation accelerate; prioritisation, customer insight and accountability remain human-heavy.
* **Low-complexity web implementation / manual data entry:** 🔴 displacement risk. Do not build a career around tasks that can be specified and checked by increasingly capable tools.

## 9. Career roadmaps: top five

### A. Backend / distributed systems + cloud/AI (recommended)

* **0–3 months:** Python or Java/Go; Git; Linux; SQL; HTTP; DSA. Build a tested REST service with PostgreSQL. Do not spend months on five frontend frameworks.
* **3–6 months:** authentication, queues, caching, Docker, CI/CD, testing, basic AWS/Azure/GCP. Build an event-driven service with metrics and a failure-mode document.
* **6–12 months:** Kafka, Redis, Kubernetes basics, load testing, system design, observability. Build a multi-service project with a deployable runbook and cost estimate.
* **6–12 months portfolio:** production-style README, architecture diagram, tests, CI, dashboards, threat model and post-incident write-up.
* **12–18 months:** apply to junior backend, software, platform, QA automation and cloud roles in Nepal/India; seek internship, open source or paid delivery work.
* **1–3 years:** own a service, on-call rotation, data migration or scaling project; add applied AI/RAG only when you can measure quality and cost.
* **3–7 years:** senior/staff backend, platform architect, AI infrastructure or security engineering; target US/global product companies or high-value remote contracts.

### B. Cloud/platform/SRE + DevSecOps

Linux and networking first; then Terraform, one cloud, Docker/Kubernetes, CI/CD, Prometheus/Grafana, IAM and incident response. Build a “broken production” lab and document recovery. Consider AWS Solutions Architect Associate, Azure Administrator or Google Associate Cloud Engineer; never treat the certificate as proof of operating skill.

### C. Data engineering + MLOps

SQL and data modelling first; then Python, Airflow, Spark, Kafka, warehouse/lake concepts, dbt, Docker and cloud storage. Build a pipeline with late data, schema changes, tests, lineage and cost controls. Add MLflow, model serving and evaluation only after the data pipeline is reliable.

### D. Cybersecurity + cloud security

Networking, Linux, identity and secure coding; then OWASP, threat modelling, SIEM, detection engineering, cloud IAM, incident response and MITRE ATT&CK. Build a home lab with logs and detection rules; write a professional incident report. Security+ or equivalent foundational certification can help; cloud-security certification becomes useful after hands-on experience.

### E. Applied ML / GenAI engineering

Python, probability, linear algebra, statistics, classical ML and software engineering; then PyTorch, transformers, retrieval, evaluation, prompt/version management, inference serving and MLOps. Build an evaluation harness, not only a chatbot. Avoid claiming research expertise from API wrappers. A master’s helps for research-heavy roles but is not required for applied engineering.

## 10. Nepal → India → global strategy

| Route | Advantages | Disadvantages / cost | Realistic timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nepal → Indian company → global company | Fastest way to gain scale, process and international-client evidence; large employer pool | Relocation/visa, competition, variable project quality and CTC opacity | 2–5 years to become globally competitive |
| Nepal → remote international job | No relocation; income upside; direct global network | Hardest without experience; contractor/tax/benefit risk; timezone and trust barriers | Often 1–3 years to first credible contract |
| Nepal → master’s abroad → global tech | Access to local internships, recruiting and immigration pathways | High tuition/living cost, visa risk, opportunity cost | 2–4 years; outcome depends heavily on institution and work experience |
| Nepal → open source/research → global | Public proof of ability and network; useful for AI/systems | Slow, unpaid and uncertain; requires sustained depth | 1–4 years |
| Nepal → startup/freelancing → global clients | Business upside and practical delivery experience | Income volatility, sales burden and weak mentorship | 1–5 years; highly variable |

**Best risk-adjusted sequence:** Nepal internship or junior role → deliberately choose production ownership → open source/public technical writing → apply to India/global roles at 12–24 months → specialise at 2–4 years. A master’s is worth considering when it materially improves work authorisation, recruiting access or research capability—not simply as an escape from an unfocused portfolio.

## 11. Emerging careers, 2027–2035: trend vs hype

### High-confidence growth

* AI inference and accelerator infrastructure; GPU scheduling, networking, compilers and cost/performance engineering.
* MLOps, evaluation, data quality, model governance and AI security.
* Cloud infrastructure, platform engineering, reliability and software supply-chain security.
* Cybersecurity for identity, cloud, APIs, AI systems and critical infrastructure.
* Semiconductor design, verification, packaging and hardware–software co-design.

### Promising but narrower

* Robotics/autonomy: real value, but geographically concentrated and hardware-capital intensive.
* AI agents: likely a product pattern and engineering capability, not a stable standalone job title; durable skills are tool integration, evaluation, security and workflows.
* Quantum computing: genuine research and specialised engineering, but not a prudent primary plan for most graduates over the next 2–3 years.
* Energy/compute infrastructure: important, but fewer accessible entry roles from a general IT degree.

## 13. Direct answer: the one path I would choose in Nepal

### Career

**Backend/distributed-systems engineer, deliberately adding cloud/platform, security and applied AI/MLOps.**

### Why

It has the best combination of accessible first jobs, employer breadth, remote portability, international transferability and long-term ability to move into high-paying AI infrastructure, platform or security roles. It also avoids a fragile bet on a fashionable title. AI may reduce the amount of routine code, but companies still need people who understand requirements, data, failure modes, security, performance, deployment and accountability.

### Skills and technologies

* **Primary:** Java or Go for backend depth; Python for automation/AI; SQL; Git; Linux.
* **Systems:** HTTP, networking, concurrency, databases, caching, queues, distributed systems and system design.
* **Operations:** Docker, one of AWS/Azure/GCP, Terraform, Kubernetes basics, CI/CD, Prometheus/Grafana and incident response.
* **AI multiplier:** embeddings/retrieval, model APIs, evaluation, inference cost, MLflow and secure data handling.
* **Security:** authentication/authorisation, secrets, OWASP, IAM, threat modelling and supply-chain hygiene.

### Projects to build

1. A multi-tenant payments-like ledger API with idempotency, PostgreSQL transactions, auth, tests and threat model.
2. An event-driven order/notification platform with Kafka or an equivalent queue, retries, dead-letter handling and observability.
3. A production-style AI retrieval service with an evaluation set, citations, latency/cost dashboard and prompt-injection tests.
4. A Kubernetes deployment with CI/CD, infrastructure-as-code, alerting, backup/restore and a post-incident report.

### Certifications worth considering

One cloud associate certification after practical labs; Security+ or equivalent if security becomes the focus; Kubernetes certification only after operating clusters. Do not collect certificates as a substitute for shipped systems.

### Companies to target

Start with Deerwalk, Cotiviti, Verisk Nepal, LogPoint, Fusemachines, EB Pearls, Braindigit, F1Soft/Khalti and WorldLink where the team offers real engineering ownership. In India target Persistent, Zoho, Jio, PhonePe, TCS, Infosys, Wipro and HCLTech according to role quality. Later target AWS, Microsoft, Google, IBM, Oracle, Salesforce, NVIDIA and Palo Alto Networks.

### Timeline and salary progression

* **0–6 months:** foundation and first serious project; no salary assumption.
* **6–12 months:** two production-style projects, internship applications and interview preparation.
* **12–18 months:** first role; Nepal market bands commonly fall around NPR 0.3–0.8m for entry software roles, but reliable public data is unavailable and employer variance is high.
* **18–36 months:** own a production service; seek India or export/remote roles; reported India bands for capable engineers often move into INR 1.2–3.5m depending on employer and location.
* **3–7 years:** senior/staff or specialist platform/AI/security; international total compensation can reach the ranges shown above, but immigration, company tier, location and equity dominate outcomes.

### Biggest risks

* Building only tutorial CRUD apps without tests, operations or measurable performance.
* Chasing every new AI framework and never learning databases, networking or Linux.
* Treating remote work as an entitlement rather than proving reliability and written communication.
* Accepting a service role with no learning or ownership for too long; switch when the project stops adding evidence.

### Alternative

**Cloud/platform engineering with security** is the best alternative if you enjoy operations more than application development. It has strong global demand, but you must accept on-call work and a steeper early learning curve.

### Avoid wasting time on

* “Prompt engineer” as a standalone career plan.
* Manual-only testing, repetitive data entry or low-complexity web work as a long-term identity.
* Certificate accumulation without labs, references or shipped code.
* Copying AI-generated code you cannot explain.
* Premature entrepreneurship before you can reliably deliver and maintain software.

### Limitations

* This is a decision-support report, not a guarantee of hiring or salary.
* Public data is much stronger for the US than Nepal; Nepal employer-level salary and vacancy data are often not publicly disclosed.
* Company presence counts are analytical counts of recurring role families, not job-opening counts.
* “AI resistance” is an informed forecast; no 5–10 year forecast can be verified today.
* Salary ranges exclude personal tax, cost of living, benefits, equity vesting and visa constraints.
* The report was prepared on 9 October 2026; readers should re-check the linked career pages before applying.
